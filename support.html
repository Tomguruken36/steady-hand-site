Common questions

How do I play?
Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer. Every row of obstacles leaves a gap: find it. The longer you drive, the higher your score.
What does COFFEE do?
The COFFEE meter keeps the driver awake; let it run out and his eyes start closing. Tap COFFEE to top it up. If his hands start shaking, the steering gets twitchy too, so do something about it.
Why is the steering loose?
Rain and snow make the road slippery, so the wheel responds more slowly. Steer early.
How do I pause?
Tap the pause button in the top-left corner. The game also pauses by itself if you leave the app or get a call.
How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Use the SOUND button on the start screen, or the SOUND and VIBRATION buttons in the pause menu.
Will I lose my records?
Records are saved on your device. They stay through updates, but deleting the app deletes them.
